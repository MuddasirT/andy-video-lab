# Sustainable Research & Production Business Plan

## Mission
Andy Video Lab is a separate, expandable research and production system for **original** kids' cartoons, educational videos, storytelling, cinematic animation and content production. A long-term objective is sustainable revenue that can fund further R&D.

## Revenue channels
1. **TinyToon Planet**: original child-friendly Shorts and longer animated compilations. Accurate Made-for-Kids classification is mandatory.
2. **Educational animation**: original explainer clips and curriculum-aligned animations for adults, educators and organizations.
3. **Original stories**: family-friendly storytelling, where each story is reviewed for originality and rights.
4. **Commissioned video production**: commercial services only using assets and model weights with appropriate commercial licenses.

## Working principles
- Free-first local research on an Apple M4 with 24GB; no paid subscriptions or services without explicit approval.
- Do not guarantee views, monetization or profit.
- Record rights and licenses for model weights, generated assets, music, voices, sound effects and source material.
- Human review for children's safety and output quality.
- Avoid mass-produced near-identical videos or repetitive low-quality template content.
- Each release must be attributable to the correct project/brand.
- Do not use unlicensed copyrighted characters or imitate real people's voices without rights/consent.
- Track actual time, render duration, disk usage, expenditures, revenue and platform compliance.

## First 90 days: objectives, not promises
- Days 1–30: validate end-to-end renderer; benchmark candidate local models; produce 10–20 original Shorts.
- Days 31–60: compare retention, production time and character consistency; test education/storytelling formats.
- Days 61–90: explore eligible monetization paths and paid client commissions; document revenue versus expense.

## Financial KPIs
Monthly: revenue, paid costs, net contribution, hours invested, videos produced, approved published videos, median render duration, mean production time, average view duration, retention, subscribers and leads. Use actual observations; do not fabricate earnings projections.
